Activities

Things to do once you arrive.

Half-day rides or multi-day treks between villages. Beginners welcome; guides provide Basotho ponies and helmets.

Where Malealea, Semonkong

Trails range from easy valley walks to full-day mountain climbs. Carry water and warm layers, since weather changes fast.

Where Ts'ehlanyane, Thaba Bosiu

Equipment rental and lessons are available at Afriski when snow is on the ground, usually June to August.

Where Afriski

The commercial abseil at Maletsunyane Falls is one of the longest single-drop abseils in the world.

Where Semonkong

Visit Thaba Bosiu, learn about King Moshoeshoe I, and see traditional crafts such as woven hats and mats.

Where Maseru, Thaba Bosiu

Best time to visit

  • Summer (Oct to Mar): green valleys, waterfalls at full flow
  • Autumn (Apr to May): clear skies, ideal for hiking
  • Winter (Jun to Aug): snow, skiing, cold nights
