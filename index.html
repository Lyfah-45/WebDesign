Why Lesotho?

Lesotho is a small, fully landlocked country inside South Africa, known for high mountains, Basotho culture and quiet trails.

  • Pony trekking between mountain villages
  • Skiing and snowboarding in winter
  • Affordable lodges, from backpackers to mountain resorts
  • Warm hosts and traditional Basotho blankets and hats
