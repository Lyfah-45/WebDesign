Maletsunyane Falls

A 192 m single-drop waterfall, one of the highest in southern Africa. Abseil it, hike to the base or watch from the rim.

Best: Oct to Apr
MountainsMokhotlong

Sani Pass

A steep gravel road climbing to about 2,876 m, ending at the highest pub in Africa.

4x4 only

Thaba Bosiu

The mountain stronghold where King Moshoeshoe I founded the Basotho nation in 1824.

Guided tours

Afriski Mountain Resort

The only ski resort in southern Africa. Snow season runs roughly June to August.

Winter fun

Boat trips
